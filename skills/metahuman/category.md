# MetaHuman

MetaHuman digital humans in Unreal Engine 5.9: authoring them with MetaHuman Creator (the in-editor Character asset, Mesh to MetaHuman, cloud auto-rigging), producing facial animation offline with MetaHuman Animator (capture data ingest, Identity, Performance, audio-driven animation), streaming facial animation live through Live Link and Live Link Hub, and shipping them in a game (run time, low-end and web targets: a face AnimBP that plays baked clips, blinks, groom and skeletal LODs, cook size, the mobile-path look, wardrobe and accessories).

Skills here cover the pipeline from footage or audio to an AnimSequence playing on an assembled MetaHuman in a packaged build.
